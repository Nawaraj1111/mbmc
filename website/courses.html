Intermediate (+2)

The candidates interested to apply for the admission in Madan Bhandari Memorial College must have certain requirements. The applicants applying for science faculty in +2 must have scored at least 60% in SLC and the students with 50% of score in SLC can apply for other +2 level faculties. If the requirements are reached the candidates can apply for further admission process and have to take entrance and oral test held by the institution.

Bachelors and Masters Level

The candidates willing to apply for the Bachelors and Masters level at Madan Bhandari Memorial College must have all the required documents for the admission process. The candidates must submit all the required documents with completely filled application form for admission. The candidates then have to go through the entrance and oral tests and only the eligible students after both tests can get admission in the university.

SCIENCE

A two-year program designed for students interested in the fundamentals of computer science and technology. Covers subjects like programming, database management, and basic networking, laying the groundwork for advanced studies in technology. Ideal for students aiming for a career in IT or software development. Focuses on scientific principles and methodologies across key subjects such as Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics. Designed for students interested in pursuing careers in medicine, engineering, and other science-related fields. Emphasizes practical and theoretical knowledge, preparing students for higher education in scientific disciplines.

MANAGEMENT

A comprehensive course aimed at providing students with foundational knowledge in business management and related fields. Subjects include accounting, economics, business studies, and marketing. Prepares students for a career in business, entrepreneurship, or further studies in management.

HUMMANITIES

Humanities courses can include the study of history, philosophy and religion, modern and ancient languages and literature, fine and performing arts, media and cultural studies, just to name a few.
